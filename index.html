WHO WE ARE

A Newsroom Without Walls

Founded as a professional home for Lusaka's journalists, the Club exists to protect the profession, sharpen its standards, and give reporters a seat at the table when decisions about the media are made.

01 — UNITY

One Press, One Voice

We bring reporters, editors, bloggers and broadcasters from across Lusaka's newsrooms into a single professional fold.

02 — ETHICS

Standards That Hold

We champion self-regulation and a code of conduct our members help write.

03 — ADVOCACY

Freedom, Defended

We work with police, regulators, and sector partners to keep journalists free to report without fear.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Advocacy On The Record

The Club sits at the table with Zambia's media stakeholders to shape how the profession governs and protects itself.

SELF-REGULATION

Media Self-Regulation Insaka

A standing voice alongside MISA Zambia, ZUJ, PAZA and the Editors' Forum, shaping a statutory, member-led code of ethics.

SAFETY

Journalist Safety Initiative

Partnering with the Zambia Police Service and Bloggers of Zambia to protect reporters on high-risk assignments.

ACCESS

Press Access & Accreditation

Pressing for fair, consistent access to briefings, courts and public institutions for accredited members.
