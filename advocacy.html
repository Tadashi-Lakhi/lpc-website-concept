WHAT WE STAND FOR

Advocacy On The Record

The Club sits at the table with Zambia's media stakeholders to shape how the profession governs and protects itself — from ethics to safety to access.

SELF-REGULATION

Media Self-Regulation Insaka

Lusaka Press Club holds a standing seat in Zambia's Media Self-Regulation Insaka, alongside MISA Zambia, ZUJ, PAZA and the Editors' Forum.

The Insaka is shaping a statutory, member-led code of ethics — one written by journalists for journalists, rather than imposed from outside the profession.

SAFETY

Journalist Safety Initiative

The Club partners with the Zambia Police Service and Bloggers of Zambia on initiatives that protect reporters covering sensitive and high-risk stories.

This includes coordination during large public assignments, so members can report safely without fear of interference.

ACCESS

Press Access & Accreditation

We press for fair, consistent access to briefings, courts and public institutions for accredited members across print, broadcast and digital media.

Accreditation support is one of the core benefits of Club membership.

WORKING TOGETHER

Our Advocacy Partners

None of this work happens alone. The Club coordinates closely with Zambia's established media and security bodies.
