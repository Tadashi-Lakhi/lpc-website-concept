WHO WE ARE

About The Club

Lusaka Press Club is a professional home for journalists, editors and media practitioners working in and around Lusaka — built to protect the craft, sharpen its standards, and give the press a seat at the table.

OUR STORY

Why The Club Exists

Zambia's newsrooms have never lacked talent — what they've lacked is a shared professional home. Lusaka Press Club brings reporters, editors, bloggers and broadcasters together under one roof, so the profession can speak with one voice on the issues that affect every newsroom: ethics, safety, and access.

The Club works alongside Zambia's established media bodies — MISA Zambia, the Zambia Union of Journalists (ZUJ), the Press Association of Zambia (PAZA), and the Zambia Media Council (ZAMEC) — as part of a wider effort to keep the profession self-governing, safe, and credible.

OUR PILLARS

What We're Built On

01 — UNITY

One Press, One Voice

We bring reporters, editors, bloggers and broadcasters from across Lusaka's newsrooms into a single professional fold — sharing resources, contacts, and solidarity.

02 — ETHICS

Standards That Hold

We champion self-regulation and a code of conduct our members help write, so Zambian journalism answers first to its own conscience.

03 — ADVOCACY

Freedom, Defended

From newsroom safety to press access, we work with police, regulators, and sector partners to keep journalists free to report without fear.
