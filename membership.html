Membership Has Its Privileges

Open to accredited journalists, editors, bloggers and media practitioners working in and around Lusaka.

  • 01

    Press Accreditation Support

    Assistance securing recognition and access credentials for official assignments.

  • 02

    Insaka Gatherings & Networking

    Regular member insakas connecting you with editors, sources and fellow reporters citywide.

  • 03

    Training & Skills Workshops

    Short courses on ethics, digital safety, investigative reporting and media law.

  • 04

    Advocacy & Legal Support

    A collective voice — and practical support — when a member's press freedom is challenged.

    Digital Press Credential

    A member ID card with a scannable QR code, so your accreditation can be verified on the spot at any assignment.
